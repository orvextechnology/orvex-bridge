Our Testing Approach

ORVEX BRIDGE follows a structured learning and testing approach:

  • Understand the website or application.
  • Identify important user functions.
  • Test each function manually.
  • Record observed results.
  • Document problems and improvement opportunities.
  • Retest after changes when possible.
